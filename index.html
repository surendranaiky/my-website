Biography • Background

Academic Profile

I am Dr. Surendranaik Y, Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at MVJ College of Engineering, Bengaluru. I received my Ph.D. in Organic Chemistry from Kuvempu University, Shivamogga, specializing in the synthesis, characterization, and pharmacological evaluation of novel heterocyclic frameworks.

My research centers on synthetic organic chemistry and molecular modeling. By employing multi-component reactions and regio-selective cyclizations, our research develops Coumarin, Pyrazole, Triazole, and Imidazole frameworks tailored for anti-tubercular and anti-cancer applications.

In parallel, I utilize Gaussian-based Density Functional Theory (DFT), Non-Linear Optical (NLO) analysis, and Computer-Aided Drug Design (CADD) to elucidate electronic structure-activity relationships and guide rational molecular engineering.

About Me

I am Dr. Surendranaik Y, Assistant Professor in Chemistry at MVJ College of Engineering. I hold a Ph.D. in Organic Chemistry from Kuvempu University, Shivamogga, with expertise in Organic and Medicinal Chemistry. My academic journey includes qualifying CSIR-NET, GATE, and K-SET. I am passionate about teaching, mentoring, and advancing research in heterocyclic compounds, spectroscopy, and applied chemistry.
